# HelpDesk Pro mobile: user guide

HelpDesk Pro lets you raise support tickets and follow the conversation from your phone.
Everything you do here also appears on the HelpDesk Pro web app.

## Install and sign in

1. Copy the APK file to your Android phone and open it. If Android asks, allow installing apps from this source.
2. Open **HelpDesk Pro** and sign in with your email and password.
3. New customer? Tap **Create an account**. You are signed in straight away.
4. Forgot your password? Tap **Forgot password?**, then open the link in the email, or tap
   **Enter reset code** and type the code from the email with your new password.

You stay signed in when you close the app. To sign out, open **Profile → Log out**.

## The app at a glance

- **Home** shows your numbers, a chart of tickets by status, quick actions and recent tickets.
- **Tickets** lists tickets. Search by title or number, tap a status, or use the filter
  button for priority, category and dates.
- The round **+** button in the middle raises a new ticket from anywhere.
- **Help** is the Help Center: search answers before raising a ticket.
- **Profile** holds your details, password reset, notifications and log out.
- The **bell** shows unread notifications.

## For customers

**Raise a ticket:** tap **+**, enter a title and description, choose a category and a
priority, and optionally add a photo. While you type the title, matching help articles
appear. Tap **Submit ticket** to get a number such as TKT-0012.

**Follow a ticket:** open it from **Tickets**. The **Chat** tab holds the conversation,
**Details** the dates and due time, and **History** every change. When the status is
**Waiting for Customer**, the team needs more information from you: reply to continue.

**When it is resolved:** tap **Reopen** if the problem is back, or **Rate support** to give
1 to 5 stars and a comment.

## For agents

- **Tickets → Assigned to me** is your work. **Queue** lists tickets nobody owns yet: open
  one and tap **Take ticket**.
- In a ticket, tap **Change status** or **Reassign**. Turn on **Internal note** before
  sending to write a note only staff can see (it turns amber). Tap the ⚡ button to insert a
  saved reply template, or save a new one.
- In **Help**, the pencil button writes a new article. Your **Profile** has **Reports**.

## For admins

Everything agents can do, on all tickets, plus in **Profile → Workspace**:

- **Reports**: filter by dates, status, category, priority or agent, then export to Excel or PDF.
- **Users and roles**: add users, change roles, and deactivate accounts (users are never deleted).
- **Categories**: add or edit categories and choose each one's default agent.
- **Priorities and SLA**: set the response and resolution hours for each priority.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Can't reach the server"**: check your internet connection and tap **Try again**.
- **"Your session has expired"**: sign in again.
- Pull down on any list to refresh it.
